Visual Art

Daft & Mutable

Whatever it is that I've done for these last 35 years, and especially during the years spent in colleges practicing and emersing myself in artistic fare, has brought me to an understanding of the infinite.

Finite through iteration becomes infinite.

There is not end to the work to do as far as expounding on and iterating with the output of chalks, inks, dyes, and digital processing by way of light and unary to binary coercion.
